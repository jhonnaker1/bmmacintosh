The Macs' ROMs
==============

Apple's ROMs aren't free, so BMMacintosh doesn't come with any: put your
own in this folder (roms), or as .rom files in the card's root. Each Mac
needs its own, and BMMacintosh finds them by their contents (a Mac ROM's
first four bytes are its checksum), whatever the files are called:

  Mac II and Quadra (Basilisk II, a Pi 4 or 5)
      A 32-bit clean ROM of a 68030 or 68040 Mac: best a Quadra 650's or
      800's (checksum F1ACAD13, 1 MB), for System 7.1 to Mac OS 8.1 (8.1
      tested). Others: the IIci's (368CADFE, 512K), the Quadra 700's and
      900's (420DBFF3), 950's (3DC27823), 605's (FF7439EE), the Centris
      650's (F1A6F343), the LC III's (ECBBC41C). Not the Mac II's, IIx's
      or SE/30's (24-bit ROMs), nor the AV Quadras'.

  Mac Plus (umac, every Pi)
      The Mac Plus's last ROM (checksum 4D1F8172, 128K), for the System
      software of its time (System 4.3 tested). Not the Mac 128K's,
      512K's or the Plus's first two ROMs.

They can be:

  - ROM files, as they are (.rom, .bin or any name)
  - MAME's zipped ROM sets, as they are: macqd800.zip (Quadra 800, with
    the Quadra 650's ROM), maciici.zip, macqd700.zip, macplus.zip... MAME
    keeps some ROMs in their chips (the Mac Plus's in two, the IIci's in
    four): BMMacintosh puts them back together.

In the menu (F12), ROM chooses between the ones found; without a choice,
the Quadra 650's is the Mac II's, if it's there.

tools/mac_media.py (in BMMacintosh's source) writes plain ROM files from
MAME's sets, for other emulators:

  tools/mac_media.py roms ~/mame/roms -o roms

System software
---------------

The Macs start from disk images in the disks folder (or anywhere on the
card): Apple's system software isn't free either. In the menu, Hard Disks
(Mac II) or Disk (Mac Plus), then Restart the Mac.

  - Disk images (.dsk, .img, .image, .hda, .hfv): a floppy's (400K,
    800K), raw or DiskCopy 4.2, or a hard disk's, raw or with an Apple
    partition map. The Mac Plus starts from such a disk's first HFS
    partition.
  - MAME's hard disks are CHD files, which BMMacintosh can't read: MAME's
    chdman makes them raw, and so does

      tools/mac_media.py disk macos81.chd -o disks/macos81.hda
      tools/mac_media.py disk sys43fin60.chd -o disks/sys43.hda

    (both Macs start from what it makes; --volume writes the HFS volume
    alone, for other emulators). FAT32, on the SD card, takes files up
    to 4 GB.

Hold Shift while Mac OS starts to start it without its extensions: Mac
OS 8.1 from MAME's macos81.chd stops at its A/ROSE extension otherwise.
