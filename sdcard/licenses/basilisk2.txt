Basilisk II
===========

BMMacintosh's Mac II kernels include Basilisk II, the 68k Macintosh
emulator by Christian Bauer and others, from kanjitalk755's macemu
(https://github.com/kanjitalk755/macemu, commit
f532be3d3d70b70f272b52050ce0889a4025d624, BasiliskII/src).

Its 68040 CPU core (uae_cpu_2021) is ARAnyM's, from UAE, the Un*x Amiga
Emulator (Copyright 1995, 1996 Bernd Schmidt; Copyright (c) 2010 ARAnyM
dev team), and its floating point emulation is from UAE and ARAnyM, all
under the GNU General Public License version 2 or later.

BMMacintosh's src/port/ii/ follows Basilisk II's Unix front end
(src/Unix/), and extfs_ii.cpp is its extfs_unix.cpp changed for the SD
card, under the same licence.
