SheepShaver
===========

BMMacintosh's Power Mac kernels include SheepShaver, the PowerPC
Macintosh emulator by Christian Bauer, Marc Hellwig and others, from
kanjitalk755's macemu (https://github.com/kanjitalk755/macemu, commit
f532be3d3d70b70f272b52050ce0889a4025d624, SheepShaver/src, with the
parts it shares with Basilisk II).

(src/Unix/); its kpx/cpu/vm.hpp and kpx/cpu/spcflags.hpp are Kheperix's,
changed for BMMacintosh, under the same licence.
