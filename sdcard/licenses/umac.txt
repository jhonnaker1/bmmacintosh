umac
====

BMMacintosh's Mac Plus kernels include umac, the Mac 128K, 512K and Plus
emulator by Matt Evans (https://github.com/evansm7/umac, commit
b62d3c6e725ca2ac2224e710b3cee765dbc35e45). Its notice, from its README:

BMMacintosh builds umac's emulator (src/main.c, via.c, scc.c, rom.c,
disc.c) and its replacement disk driver (include/sonydrv.h), not its SDL
front end (src/unix_main.c) or the keyboard tables taken from Mini vMac
(include/keymap.h, keymap_sdl.h): BMMacintosh has its own keyboard code.
The parts from Basilisk II in disc.c and b2_macos_util.h are under the
GPL version 2 or later, as Basilisk II is (basilisk2.txt, GPL-2.0.txt).
umac's replacement Mac disk driver (macsrc/sonydrv.S, include/sonydrv.h),
whose changes are marked "GPLv2", isn't used: BMMacintosh has its own,
from Basilisk II's (GPL version 2 or later).
